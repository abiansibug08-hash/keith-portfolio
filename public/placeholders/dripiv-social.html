Social Content & Marketing Campaign

How I grew DripIV Therapy's audience and drove appointment inquiries through consistent social content, branded graphics, and short-form video — building credibility for an IV therapy clinic in a competitive wellness market.

ClientDripIV Therapy
ChannelsInstagram · Facebook · TikTok
FocusSocial · Content · Video

01 The challenge

DripIV Therapy needed to establish credibility and grow awareness in a wellness niche where potential clients had plenty of questions and low awareness of IV therapy benefits. The goal was to educate the audience, build trust, and convert followers into booked appointments through a consistent mix of educational and promotional content.

02 Content workflow

Each piece of content followed a repeatable production process built for speed and consistency.

Topic & trend research→ Script or caption outline→ Graphic or video production→ Edit & brand check→ Publish & engage

03 Campaign types

Content was built around four formats, each targeting a different stage of the client journey.

Educational

IV Therapy Explainers

Carousel and single-image posts explaining the benefits of IV drips, hydration therapy, and vitamin infusions. Designed to answer common questions and overcome hesitation before booking.

Promotions

Service Offers & Packages

Branded promotional graphics for seasonal deals, new service launches, and package offers. Clear CTAs with booking links to drive direct appointment inquiries from social.

Short-Form Video

Reels & TikTok Content

Short behind-the-scenes clips, treatment walkthroughs, and client testimonial edits produced in CapCut. Optimized for Reels and TikTok to reach new audiences beyond existing followers.

Engagement Posts

Wellness Tips & Lifestyle

Wellness tips, hydration facts, and lifestyle content that kept the brand visible between promotions. Built community and positioned DripIV as a trusted voice in the wellness space.

04 What was built

  • Monthly social content calendar across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok
  • Branded Canva template set — service graphics, educational carousels, and promo posts
  • Short-form video content edited in CapCut: Reels, TikToks, and service walkthroughs
  • Educational content series covering IV therapy benefits, ingredients, and FAQs
  • Seasonal and service-based promotional campaigns with direct booking CTAs
  • Hashtag and caption strategy targeted at local wellness and health-conscious audiences

05 Results

Consistent content presence across all channels with growing audience engagement.
