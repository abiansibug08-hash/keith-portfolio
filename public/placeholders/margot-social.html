Social Media · Brand & Design

Brand & Social Media Campaign

How I built and maintained the visual identity and social media presence for Margot European Spa — consistent branded content across Instagram and Facebook that reflected the spa's luxury positioning.

ClientMargot European Spa
ChannelsInstagram · Facebook
FocusBrand · Social · Design

01 The challenge

Margot European Spa needed a consistent, elevated brand presence on social media that matched the experience they offered in-person — luxury, calm, and professional. The goal was to attract new clients and keep regulars engaged between visits with content that felt on-brand every time it appeared in their feed.

02 Content workflow

Every post followed a repeatable system to keep the feed cohesive and on-schedule.

Monthly theme planning→ Graphic design in Canva→ Copy & caption writing→ Client review→ Schedule & publish

03 Campaign types

Content was organized around four recurring campaign formats, each serving a different goal.

Brand Identity

Visual Consistency

Designed branded templates in Canva using the spa's color palette, typography, and tone. Applied across every post type to keep the feed recognizable and polished.

Promotions

Service & Seasonal Offers

Promotional graphics for seasonal packages, holiday gift cards, and featured treatments. Designed to drive bookings with clear CTAs while staying true to the brand aesthetic.

Engagement Content

Educational & Lifestyle Posts

Skincare tips, treatment spotlights, and behind-the-scenes content. Designed to build trust and position Margot as the go-to local expert in European skincare and wellness.

Graphic Design

Custom Marketing Assets

Print-ready and digital promotional materials including flyers, in-spa signage, and story graphics. Maintained the same visual language across digital and physical touchpoints.

04 What was built

  • Branded Canva template library — covers, carousels, story frames, and promotional graphics
  • Monthly social content calendar with consistent posting cadence across Instagram and Facebook
  • Promotional campaign graphics for seasonal and service-based offers
  • Educational content series: skincare tips and treatment spotlights
  • Custom print and digital marketing assets for in-spa use
  • Hashtag strategy and caption framework tailored to the spa's audience

05 Results

Consistent content output and a unified brand voice across all social channels.
