AI Search & Social Content Plan

How I structured content for KKRG / REALIV to rank on Google and get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews — alongside ongoing social media campaigns across Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube.

ClientKKRG / REALIV
Duration4.5 Years
FocusSEO · GEO/AEO · Social

01 The challenge

KKRG / REALIV needed to grow search visibility in a competitive Scottsdale real estate market and stay visible as more buyers shifted to asking AI tools instead of typing keywords into Google. Their existing site had thin content, no schema markup, and low engagement on social — no system to generate consistent leads from organic channels.

02 Content workflow

Every piece of content followed the same production loop — built for both Google crawlers and AI citation engines.

Keyword + AI query research→ Topic outline→ Draft (AI-assisted)→ Schema markup→ Publish + distribute→ Track in GSC + AI tools

03 Campaign types

Content was split across four active campaign streams, each targeting a different channel and intent.

SEO Content

Buyer & Seller Guides

Long-form guides targeting transactional real estate keywords in Scottsdale — "how to buy a home in Scottsdale," "best neighborhoods in Scottsdale AZ." Structured with H2/H3 headers and FAQ schema to capture featured snippets and AI Overviews.

GEO / AEO

AI Citation Content

Short Q&A-format articles structured around the exact phrasing people ask ChatGPT and Perplexity — "who is the best realtor in Scottsdale," "what is the average home price in Scottsdale." Goal: get cited as a source in AI-generated answers.

Social Media

Instagram & Facebook

Weekly content calendar: listing reveals, market update graphics, client spotlights, and branded Reels. Designed in Canva, video clips edited in Premiere and CapCut. Scheduled via Meta Business Suite.

Email Campaigns

Drip & Newsletter

Monthly market update newsletters via ActivePipe and Mailchimp. Drip sequences for new leads with automated follow-up at day 1, 3, 7, and 14. Content repurposed from blog posts to keep production efficient.

04 What was built

  • Full SEO content calendar — 2 to 4 posts per month, planned 6 weeks ahead
  • FAQ schema library covering the top 30 buyer and seller questions for Scottsdale
  • GEO/AEO article bank targeting AI search queries — structured for citation in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
  • Social content system: branded templates, monthly themes, and a repeatable posting schedule across Instagram and Facebook
  • YouTube strategy with VidIQ keyword research for property tour and neighborhood guide videos
  • Email drip sequence (5 steps) for new buyer leads, built in ActivePipe and Mailchimp
  • Monthly reporting dashboard covering Google Search Console, GA4, and social analytics
